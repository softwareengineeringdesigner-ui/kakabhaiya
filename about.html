Model Town D-Block Walay Roots

How Bhaiya BBQ (Model Town D-Block Walay) Sparked a Barbecue Revolution Since 1970

It all began in 1970 in the historic culinary heart of Bhaiya BBQ (Model Town D-Block Walay), Lahore. The founders ignited a pit of glowing acacia charcoal and threaded hand-minced beef skewers seasoned with ancestral Punjabi spices. Word traveled fast: here was barbecue cooked with soul, patience, and honor.

While others turned to gas broilers and artificial flavorings, Bhaiya BBQ stayed devoted to pure hardwood coals, raw green papaya marination, and whole spices stone-ground every morning. That uncompromising standard became Lahore's golden benchmark.

Today, that unbroken 56-year legacy continues at our dedicated dining destination: 414-E, Hoo Chowk, Johar Town, Lahore. Operating proudly under parent brand Kaka's Life (led by Brand Owner & CEO Hafiz Muhammad Mubeen), we welcome you to taste barbecue heritage at its finest.

Our Creed

The Four Unbreakable Commandments

Why dining at Kaka Bhaiya is a culinary guarantee of purity.

100% Certified Halal Prime Sourcing

Every gram of mutton and beef is sourced daily from registered pastoral farms with transparent verification.

Real Acacia Coal Only

We never compromise with gas broilers or liquid smoke flavoring. Authentic barbecue requires authentic firewood embers.

Zero Artificial Chemical Tenderizers

Our meat becomes silky-soft through organic raw green papaya marination and aged yogurt curing for a minimum of 18 hours.

Zero Fat Re-Use Policy

Desi ghee, pure animal fat, and cooking oils are never reused or stored overnight. Fresh batches for every meal session.
