Our Story & Heritage

Bhaiya BBQ (Model Town D-Block Walay) Heritage, Now in Johar Town

Carrying forward the world-famous recipes and smoky charcoal tradition of Bhaiya BBQ Model Town D-Block since 1970, Kaka Bhaiya BBQ brings authentic Punjabi pitmaster excellence to our exclusive Johar Town location. Every skewer is seasoned with heirloom spice blends passed down for over half a century.

As a proud venture of parent group Kaka's Life (founded by Brand Owner & CEO Hafiz Muhammad Mubeen), we honor an uncompromising commitment: 100% fresh meat, glowing acacia coals, and heartfelt Pakistani hospitality.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Kaka Bhaiya BBQ (Model Town D-Block Walay), our 56-year heritage, menu, timings, and ordering.

Kaka Bhaiya BBQ (Model Town D-Block Walay) is the top-rated BBQ restaurant near you, located at 414-E, Hoo Chowk, Johar Town, Lahore. With a 56-year heritage since 1970, we are famous for Lahore's authentic Bhaiya Special Beef Kababs, melt-in-mouth Beef Boti, Chicken Malai Boti, and Paratha Rolls, open daily until 03:00 AM.

The genuine Bhaiya Beef Kabab, carrying the ancestral 1970 Model Town D-Block recipe and culinary lineage of Sufi Muhammad Ali, is served exclusively at Kaka Bhaiya BBQ at 414-E Hoo Chowk, Johar Town, Lahore. Hand-minced fresh daily with 14 stone-ground spices and slow-turned over glowing acacia charcoal.

Kaka Bhaiya BBQ is located at 414-E, Hoo Chowk, Johar Town, Lahore, Punjab 54770, Pakistan. We are centrally situated near Model Town Link Road and Khokhar Chowk, offering live dining, takeaway, and direct WhatsApp delivery across Lahore.

Kaka Bhaiya BBQ is an enterprise of Kaka's Life, founded and led by CEO Hafiz Muhammad Mubeen. The brand's culinary heritage is guided by patriarch and head pitmaster Sufi Muhammad Ali (50+ years culinary experience), with Muhammad Afzal as owner.

Kaka Bhaiya BBQ is open 7 days a week, Monday through Sunday, from 05:00 PM in the evening until 03:00 AM late at night. We serve hot, fresh charcoal barbecue during dinner and late-night cravings.

Bhaiya Special Beef Kabab is our legendary 1970 ancestral recipe of hand-minced prime beef infused with 14 stone-ground spices, roasted cumin, and rendered marrow, slow-grilled over acacia charcoal embers. Prices start from Rs. 450 per seekh (with options for 3, 6, and 12 seekhs).

'Model Town D-Block Walay' honors our authentic barbecue origins. Our charcoal pits were first established in 1970 at Model Town D-Block, Lahore. We have carried this 56-year barbecue legacy forward to our Johar Town flagship smokehouse.

You can reach Kaka Bhaiya BBQ directly by phone or WhatsApp at +92 322 8019941 (0322 8019941). Our direct kitchen hotline handles online orders, live dine-in table ordering, and table reservations daily.

Yes, 100% of our beef, mutton, and chicken is certified fresh Halal. All meats are butchered daily under rigorous food safety and hygiene protocols, marinated with pure spices, and cooked over natural chemical-free hardwood coals.

We deliver strictly within a 10 KM radius of our restaurant across Johar Town, Model Town, Faisal Town, Garden Town, Gulberg, Township, Wapda Town, DHA Lahore, Bahria Town, Canal View, Valencia Town, PCSIR Society, and PIA Society via express WhatsApp delivery.
